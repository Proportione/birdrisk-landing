01The BirdRisk apps (iOS and Android)

BirdRisk is free: the apps have no purchases and no subscriptions. They collect only what they need to show you bird-strike risk:

  • No account — the apps do not ask for your name, email or a password. On first launch they obtain an anonymous session token from our backend, kept securely on the device (encrypted, in the Keychain on iOS). It is not linked to your identity.
  • Location — with your permission, the apps use your device location to centre the risk map on your area and to show your position in flight. This location stays on your device, unless you turn on the optional flight-track sharing described below. We do not use it to track you across apps or services, and we do not sell it.
  • Flight feedback — after a flight of 5 minutes or more, the app may ask once whether the map matched what you saw. Answering is optional. If you answer, we receive your answer (yes or no), the reasons you pick, any comment you write (optional, up to 500 characters), the start and end time of the flight, the highest risk band shown during it, which calibration the map used, the platform and the app version, and a random identifier created for that single flight so that a resend is not counted twice. We do not store your identity, your session token or your device identifier with it.
  • Optional flight track — only if you turn on “Share a simplified flight track with feedback” in Settings (off by default), your feedback also includes a simplified track of that flight: at most 200 points, rounded to about 11 metres, altitude rounded to 100 ft, and times counted from the start of the flight. You can turn it off at any time; it then stops being sent.
  • Diagnostics — in the iOS app we use Firebase Analytics (configured without the advertising identifier / IDFA) and Crashlytics to understand aggregate usage and to fix crashes. Analytics events never include your location, your flight track or your comments. This is not used for advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so no App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown. The Android app does not send analytics or crash reports.

BirdRisk shows bird-strike risk information only — it informs, it does not advise, and it is not a navigation or flight-safety system (see our Terms of Use).

02Website and waitlist

On the website we only collect personal information — such as your name, email address, phone number and IP address — when you voluntarily provide it, for example by requesting a country availability notification or subscribing to BirdRisk product updates. Product updates use a separate hosted Brevo form with your email address and explicit consent, followed by email confirmation. A country availability request does not subscribe you to product updates. These emails are handled through our email provider Brevo (Sendinblue SAS), acting as a data processor on our behalf. You can cancel product updates at any time using the unsubscribe link in our newsletters. The website uses static pages; any cookies are used only to improve your experience and for site analysis — never to track you across sites or for advertising.

03How we use your information

We use information exclusively for the purposes expressed when it is collected: to provide and secure the app, to send the country availability notification or product updates you have separately requested, to fix problems and improve the service, and to respond to your enquiries. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for requested emails and analytics, and the performance of our contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) to provide the app to you. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Flight feedback is used only to check and improve the calibration of the risk map. The legal basis for flight feedback without a track is our legitimate interest in the accuracy of the map (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), which you can object to simply by not answering. The legal basis for the optional flight track is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), given with the Settings switch and withdrawable at any time with the same switch.

04Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent or transfer your personal information. We share data only with trusted providers who help us operate the service — Google/Firebase (analytics and crash reporting), Brevo (requested emails) and our hosting provider, Google Cloud — each acting as a processor bound to keep your data confidential, or to comply with the law.

05Security and retention

We implement appropriate security measures, including encryption in transit (HTTPS) and secure on-device storage of the session token. We keep personal data only for as long as we need it for the purpose you gave it — your email details while you request those emails. Unsubscribing stops newsletters; suppression information may be retained to respect your opt-out choices. You can request erasure using the privacy contacts below.

Flight tracks are deleted 90 days after we receive them. The rest of each flight feedback record is deleted 12 months after we receive it. Our hosting provider (Google Cloud, in the EU) stores this data on our behalf. Our encrypted database backups, also kept in the EU, are overwritten on their own cycle: a deleted record can remain in them for up to 14 more days.

06Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these, email us at hello@birdrisk.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — the CNPD in Portugal or the AEPD in Spain.

Because flight feedback is stored without your identity, we cannot find a specific person's records to give access to or erase them on request. You keep control by choosing whether to answer and whether the flight-track switch is on.

07Children

BirdRisk is intended for pilots and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
